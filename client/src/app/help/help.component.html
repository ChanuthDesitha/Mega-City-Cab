Help & User Guide

Login

To access the system, enter your username and password on the login page. If you forget your password, contact the administrator.

Adding a New Booking

Go to the "New Booking" section and enter the required details: customer name, address, telephone number, and destination.

Viewing Booking Details

Navigate to "Booking Details" to view a list of all bookings along with their current status.

Billing & Payment

The system calculates the total fare based on distance, applicable taxes, and discounts. Bookings can be viewed and printed from the "Billing" section.

Car & Driver Management

Admins can add, update, and remove cars and drivers from the system under "Manage Cars & Drivers."

System Navigation

  • Dashboard: View an overview of bookings and system status.
  • Bookings: Create, update, or cancel bookings.
  • Billing: Calculate and print bills for completed trips.
  • Drivers & Cars: Manage drivers and vehicle assignments.
  • Logout: Securely log out of the system.

Logout

To exit the system, click on the "Logout" button to securely log out and close the application.
